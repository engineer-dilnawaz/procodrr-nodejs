>  base64 image.png

>  echo "data:image/png;base64,$(base64 image.png)"

---
You can use the following command as well to get the base64 encoded data:

▎ base64 -i image.png

If you want the data URI of the image, you can use the following command:

▎ echo "data:image/png;base64,$(base64 -i image.png)"

To copy the result straight to your clipboard, pipe it to pbcopy:

▎ echo "data:image/png;base64,$(base64 -i image.png)" | pbcopy
