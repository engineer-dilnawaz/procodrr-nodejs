🌐 How Internet Comes to Our Home?
Internet ghar tak kaise aata hai? Chalo step-by-step dekhte hain:
1️⃣ Content Origin – Data Centers
📍 Internet ki journey start hoti hai yaha se
Websites, videos, apps — sab data centers me hosted hote hain
Ye data centers hoti hain huge server farms, jahan pe digital content store aur manage kiya jaata hai
🖥️ Example: YouTube ke videos Google ke data centers me hote hain
2️⃣ Long Distance Transmission – Submarine Cables & Satellites
🌍 Kaise data continents cross karta hai?
✅ Main method:
Submarine Fiber Optic Cables
Ye cables oceans ke niche bichhi hoti hain
99% international internet traffic inhi cables se hoti hai
📡 Alternate/Backup:
Satellites (especially remote areas ke liye ya emergency backup)
3️⃣ Internet Backbone – Tier 1 ISPs
🔗 Global Internet Highway
Submarine cables directly connect hoti hain Tier 1 ISPs se
Examples: Tata Communications, AT&T, NTT
Ye ISPs pura worldwide internet backbone operate karte hain
Inke paas huge capacity hoti hai data handle karne ki
4️⃣ Regional Distribution – Tier 2 & Tier 3 ISPs
🏙️ National/Local level distribution
Tier 2/3 ISPs directly Tier 1 providers se data lete hain
Ye companies Internet ko cities, towns, villages tak pahuchati hain
Example: Airtel, Jio, ACT, BSNL, etc.
5️⃣ Local Distribution – “Last Mile”
🚪 Neighborhood tak internet ka aana
Ab Tier 2/3 ISPs apna local infrastructure use karte hain:
Fiber optic cables
Coaxial (cable TV wires)
DSL (telephone lines)
Ye line tumhare building ya ghar tak aati hai
Isko “Last Mile” connection bola jaata hai
6️⃣ Home Connection – Modem & Router
🏠 Ghar ke andar internet ka setup
Jo wire ghar tak aayi hai, wo connect hoti hai Modem se
Modem signal ko digital data me convert karta hai
Modem ke baad aata hai Router
Router tumhare ghar me Wi-Fi create karta hai
Ye sab devices ko connect karta hai
7️⃣ Device Access – Internet on Your Devices
📱💻📺 Finally tumhare devices online!
Devices (mobile, laptop, TV) Wi-Fi router se connect hote hain
Router tumhare device ko IP address assign karta hai
Jab bhi tum koi website open karte ho ya app use karte ho:
Request jaati hai router → ISP → Tier 1 → Data Center
Waha se data return hota hai same path follow karke
