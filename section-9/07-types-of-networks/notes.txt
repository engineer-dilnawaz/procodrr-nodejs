🌐 Types of Network
Networks ko unke size, range, aur usage ke basis pe divide kiya jata hai.

🔹 1. LAN (Local Area Network)
📌 Definition:
Chhoti jagah mein banaya gaya network — jaise ek ghar, school, ya office ke andar.
🏠 Example:
Office ke sare computers ek router se connected ho
Ghar ka WiFi network
📏 Range:
Few meters to a few kilometers
⚙️ Use:
Fast, secure, aur low-cost network for internal communication

🔹 2. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)
📌 Definition:
Ek city level ka network — multiple LANs ko jodta hai within a city.
🏙️ Example:
City-wide broadband services
Cable TV network
📏 Range:
Up to 50 km (approx.)
⚙️ Use:
City level communication — schools, colleges, and government offices in one city

🔹 3. WAN (Wide Area Network)
📌 Definition:
Bahut bade area mein phela hua network — countries aur continents tak.
🌍 Example:
The Internet itself
Bank ATMs network across India
📏 Range:
Thousands of kilometers
⚙️ Use:
Global communication, large-scale business connectivity

🧩 Other Types (Special Purpose Networks)

🔸 PAN (Personal Area Network)
📌 Definition:
Ek individual person ke around banaya gaya very short range network
📱 Example:
Bluetooth devices
Tethering from mobile
Smartwatch connecting to phone
📏 Range:
~10 meters

🔸 BAN (Body Area Network)
📌 Definition:
Ek human body ke aas-paas bana network — mainly healthcare ke liye
🩺 Example:
Heart rate monitor
Fitness tracker syncing to mobile app
📏 Range:
Few centimeters to meters

🔸 CAN (Campus Area Network)
📌 Definition:
Ek campus mein (college, university, company) multiple buildings ke andar LANs ka combination.
🏫 Example:
IIT campus
Company campus with multiple buildings
📏 Range:
Few kilometers, but larger than LAN and smaller than MAN
